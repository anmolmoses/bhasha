# Simple Guide - One-Tap Translation

## ✨ Now Super Easy! Just ONE Button!

Your app now works with **just ONE tap** - no more copying, pasting, or multiple steps!

## How It Works Now

### Step 1: Setup (Do Once)

1. Open Bhasha app
2. Go to Settings (gear icon)
3. Turn ON "Floating bubble active"
4. **Important**: Tap "Enable accessibility" button
5. In the settings that open, find "Bhasha" and turn it ON

### Step 2: Using It (Every Time)

1. Open WhatsApp, Messages, or any app
2. Tap in the text box and type your message in Kannada
3. **Tap the blue "T" button** (floating on your screen)
4. **Done!** Your Kannada text automatically becomes English!
5. The phone also **reads the English out loud**, so you can hear it too

### Don't Want to Type? Just Speak!

1. Tap in the text box
2. **Press and HOLD** the blue button — it turns red and listens
3. Say your message in Kannada
4. Let go — your message appears in English!

### Can't Read an English Message? Double-Tap!

First, ask your family to turn on "Double-tap screen translation" in Settings (one time only).

1. Open the message you can't read
2. **Tap the blue button TWICE quickly**
3. If the phone asks to allow screen capture, tap Allow
4. Kannada appears on top of the English — read it!
5. Tap anywhere to go back

Sometimes this takes a few seconds — that's normal, just wait.

Type in English instead, and the same button gives you Kannada. You never have
to tell it which way round — it works that out from what you typed.

## Want a different language?

Look at the small dark chip just under the blue button. It says something like
**KN⇄EN**. That is your language pair.

**Tap the chip** and a list opens right there, on top of WhatsApp. Pick the
language you want and carry on. You never have to leave the app you are in.

## What the Button Does Automatically

When you tap the blue "T" button:

- ✅ Reads your typed text
- ✅ Translates Kannada → English
- ✅ Replaces the text automatically
- ✅ Reads the English out loud in your chosen voice
- ✅ Shows a small **Undo** button for a few seconds

## Made a Mistake? Tap Undo!

After the text changes, a small **Undo** button appears. Tap it and your original Kannada comes right back. Nothing you type is ever lost.

## Important Notes

### The Blue "T" Button

- It floats on your screen
- You can drag it anywhere
- Works in ALL apps (WhatsApp, Messages, Email, etc.)

### It Speaks Your Language

- The app's messages appear in Kannada
- It remembers your favourite voice and how fast it speaks
- It remembers names the way YOUR family spells them

### Accessibility Permission

- **Must enable this** for one-tap to work
- It's safe - only Bhasha uses it
- Lets the app read and write text for you

### If It Doesn't Work

- Make sure you tapped in a text box first
- Check if accessibility is enabled
- Restart the app if needed

## For Children/Family: How to Help Setup

1. Install Bhasha app
2. Get a Sarvam API key from dashboard.sarvam.ai and enter it in the app (it starts with "sk_")
3. Select: Kannada → English
4. Turn ON "Floating bubble active"
5. **Enable accessibility service** (most important!)
6. Pick their voice and speaking speed in Settings
7. Show them how to tap the blue button once, and how to hold it to speak

## Practice Together

1. Open WhatsApp
2. Tap to write a new message
3. Type: "ನಾನು ಚೆನ್ನಾಗಿದ್ದೇನೆ"
4. Tap the blue "T" button
5. Watch it change to: "I am fine" — and listen to it
6. Then try holding the button and just saying it instead

Do this 2-3 times so they remember!
